# Preview image credits

Publication preview images are referenced from `_bibliography/papers.bib` via the
`preview={...}` field and rendered by `_layouts/bib.html`.

Unless listed below, images are figures from the corresponding paper.

## Third-party images

### `wigner_semicircle.png`

Used for *Sharp Bounds on Ground State Energy of the SYK Model* (arXiv:2607.27185),
which has no figure of its own suitable as a thumbnail. The plot shows the empirical
spectral density of a normalized symmetric Gaussian random matrix against Wigner's
semicircle law — the spectral edge is the quantity the paper bounds.

- **Title:** ESD of normalized Gaussian matrix size 1500x1500
- **Author:** Itamer9
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ESD_of_normalized_Gaussian_matrix_size_1500x1500.png
- **License:** [CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-21

CC0 waives the attribution requirement, but the credit is carried on
`_pages/publications.md` anyway.
